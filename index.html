Your money and your household

Your changes are saved in this browser only.
Household income What the two of you have each month. For the café, that's the café's profit after tax: it's your salary, because you both work there. For padel, it's your normal job pay plus your share of the club's profit, because the partner runs the club and you keep working in Zurich.
Extra vs normal jobs How much more (or less) you earn than if you both had normal jobs. This is the fair way to judge the café, since running it means giving up those jobs.
Months to earn back Money you invest ÷ the extra you earn each month. If the extra is zero or negative, you never earn the investment back.
Reserve Whatever is left of your budget after setup. If your household income is lower than your living costs, the reserve pays the difference, and the card shows how many months it lasts.
